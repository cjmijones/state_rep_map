# Elections, polling, and federal offices

Research checked September 26, 2026. “Voting history” includes both recorded legislative roll calls and past election outcomes. Election outcomes are results for candidates/races; they are distinct from members’ floor votes.

## Practical first release

Start with a refreshable election calendar, links to poll releases, and a historical results panel. Label polls as estimates, with sponsor and field dates. Defer a poll average until there is a dependable feed and redistribution rights. States certify results; FEC’s federal results compile state reports. Dates change, and race schedules can differ: in 2026 Alabama split primaries by Senate/CDs 3–5 and CDs 1, 2, 6, 7, while Louisiana House and Senate dates differ. Use the current FEC calendar, retain retrieval date/source, and verify dates with state election authorities. [FEC responsibilities](https://www.fec.gov/introduction-campaign-finance/election-results-and-voting-information/) · [Current 2026 calendar](https://www.fec.gov/help-candidates-and-committees/dates-and-deadlines/2026-reporting-dates/congressional-pre-election-reporting-dates-2026/) · [FEC primary-date PDF](https://www.fec.gov/documents/5910/2026pdates.pdf)

FEC candidate and campaign-finance data are useful for a prototype, not a canonical ballot. OpenFEC supports `DEMO_KEY` without signup and updates data nightly. Try `https://api.open.fec.gov/v1/candidates/?api_key=DEMO_KEY&election_year=2026&office=H&per_page=20`. Its candidate definition is tied to the federal $5,000 threshold; states control ballot access. Confirm candidates and outcomes with state sources. [OpenFEC API docs](https://api.open.fec.gov/developers) · [FEC ballot access responsibility](https://www.fec.gov/introduction-campaign-finance/election-results-and-voting-information/)

## Polling: useful, but a separate and limited data product

There is no official poll feed analogous to certified returns. Polls come from individual pollsters/media; show source, sponsor, field dates, sample, mode and methodology as estimates. Coverage is selective: AAPOR’s 2024 evaluation assembled district polling for 94 House contests, not all 435, and may rely on confidential respondent data while publishing aggregates. Pew’s poll landscape research describes fragmented pollster sites, archives and broken links, and focuses on national polling. Start with curated links; check reuse rights before republishing results/graphics. Expect absent or stale district polls. [AAPOR 2024 pre-election report](https://aapor.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/AAPOR-Task-Force-on-2024-Pre-Election-Polling_Report.pdf) · [Pew polling methodology](https://www.pewresearch.org/methods/2023/04/19/polling-landscape-methodology/)

## Federal scope, geometry, and joins

- **Current U.S. House representation:** Census’ 119th-Congress congressional districts are a suitable geometry starting point (national 1:500,000 shapefile: 6.7 MB), distinct from state legislative layers. The House says current members serve through January 2, 2027; redrawn districts can change where people vote in 2026 without changing current representation until January 2027. Keep current geometry separate from 2026 election-plan geometry; version districts by plan/election year. [Census 119th CD files](https://www.census.gov/geographies/mapping-files/2025/geo/carto-boundary-file.html) · [House redistricting note](https://www.house.gov/representatives/find-your-representative)
- **U.S. Senate:** a Senate race is statewide and seat/class-specific. Use state geometry, with one election-result record for the contested seat; do not fabricate Senate district polygons. A state may have no regular Senate race in a given cycle, or a special election. Each state has two Senate seats; an officeholder roster can show a vacancy, and a particular election outcome belongs to one seat.
- **U.S. House:** join outcome to state plus the congressional district as drawn for that election, not a state-house district or today’s district. Preserve election date, office, contest type (primary/runoff/general/special), district code (including at-large `00`), and a boundary-plan/version key. A redrawn district does not have a stable geographic identity across maps; don’t imply direct historical continuity if the lines changed.
- **State legislatures:** for state House/lower chamber and Senate/upper chamber outcomes, the same rule applies: use that state chamber’s district geography and plan vintage in force for the particular election, not the current map. Result formats and availability are state-administered, so expect per-state imports and source citations. For prior roll-call research and state-vote coverage constraints, see [voting-history.md](voting-history.md).

FEC publishes certified historical federal results as biennial files, not a live results API. Its page currently links to 2022 federal results and 2024 presidential results; recent congressional results may need state-by-state sourcing until the next FEC volume. [FEC results](https://www.fec.gov/introduction-campaign-finance/election-results-and-voting-information/)

## Cheap source probes

1. **No key, federal votes/rosters:** Senate XML index links to current-senator data and vote lists/details: [Senate XML index](https://www.senate.gov/general/XML.htm). House Clerk publishes roll-call lists and individual XML: [House roll-call index](https://clerk.house.gov/evs/2025/index.asp). See [voting-history.md](voting-history.md) for state roll calls.
2. **No key, map source:** download Census 119th congressional-district national shapefile above; a low-resolution 1:20,000,000 file is under 1 MB for a first rendering probe. Census/TIGER products are downloadable and cite the vintage, but verify a plan-specific 2026 boundary source before mapping candidates in districts redrawn during this Congress.
3. **No key, elections:** FEC calendars/results are HTML/PDF/XLS; state authorities certify local results. [EAC state election links](https://www.eac.gov/voters/voter-faqs)
4. **Demo key, candidate prototype:** the OpenFEC candidate query above needs no personal key while using `DEMO_KEY`; request a production key and respect documented quotas before scheduled refresh. [OpenFEC docs and demo-key guidance](https://api.open.fec.gov/developers)
5. **No key, polling:** link pollster/media releases; there is no complete recent district-poll API. Pew’s research microdata is released after cleaning and requires an account/terms, so it is not near-real-time. [Pew access and terms](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2021/10/22/how-to-access-pew-research-center-survey-data/)
